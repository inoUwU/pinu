# Menu Image Prompt

This is a menu image prompt for Google Nanobanana model.  

## **Meta-Prompts for Consistent Menu Photography**  

To use these, simply replace the bracketed `[PLACEHOLDER]` text with your specific details.

---

**1. Primary Food Shot (Hero Image)**

*   **Core Request:** A professional, well-lit, high-resolution food photograph of `[Dish Name]` in `[Cuisine Style]`.
*   **Dish Description:** `[Detailed description of the dish, e.g., "a steaming bowl of tonkotsu ramen with two slices of chashu pork, a perfectly soft-boiled ajitama egg, green onions, bamboo shoots, and a sheet of nori seaweed."]`
*   **Composition:** `[e.g., "Centered in the frame, slightly angled from above, showcasing all key ingredients clearly."]`
*   **Lighting:** `[e.g., "Bright, even, soft studio lighting from the front and slightly above, minimizing harsh shadows."]`
*   **Background:** `[e.g., "Pure white seamless background, clean and uncluttered, isolating the dish."]`
*   **Bowl/Plateware:** `[e.g., "Elegant, simple ceramic bowl with a subtle blue pattern (if applicable), or a minimalist white plate."]`
*   **Garnish/Detail:** `[e.g., "Highlight steam rising gently from the bowl, fresh and vibrant garnishes."]`
*   **Overall Feel:** `[e.g., "Appetizing, clean, modern, focus on freshness and quality."]`
